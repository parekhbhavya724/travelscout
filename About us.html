What is TravelScout?

Planning a trip can mean opening dozens of tabs, comparing itineraries, searching for things to do and wondering whether anyone else is going to the same place.

TravelScout brings all of that together in one place. Our platform combines AI-powered recommendations, itinerary matching and a people finder designed especially for travelers who want to explore without necessarily traveling alone.
